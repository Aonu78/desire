Order Processing

  • Orders are processed within 24-48 hours
  • You'll receive a confirmation email once your order is placed
  • Tracking number will be provided via email and SMS once shipped
  • Orders placed on weekends are processed on Monday

Tracking Your Order

SMS Alert

Online Tracking

Track on our website or courier's portal

Delivery Information

  • A valid phone number is required for delivery coordination
  • Our delivery partner will call before delivery
  • If you're not available, they'll attempt delivery again the next day
  • After 3 failed attempts, the order will be returned to us

Cash on Delivery

For Cash on Delivery orders:

  • There is a 5% advance payment for cash on delivery parcels.
  • If the buyer does not receive the parcel, the seller incurs the loss of carrier charges.
  • If you are not satisfied with the delivery, Adult item will push you and dispatch you with your consent.
