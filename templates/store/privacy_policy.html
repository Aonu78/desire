Introduction

At Desiretoys, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or make a purchase.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name and contact information (email, phone number)
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Payment information (processed securely)
  • Order history and preferences

Automatic Information

  • IP address and browser type
  • Device information
  • Cookies and usage data

How We Use Your Information

  • Process your orders and payments
  • Communicate with you about your orders
  • Send order updates and tracking information
  • Improve our website and services
  • Prevent fraud and enhance security

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Delivery partners (for shipping)
  • Payment processors (for transactions)
  • Legal authorities (when required by law)

Data Security

We implement various security measures to protect your personal information:

  • SSL encryption for data transmission
  • Secure servers and firewalls
  • Regular security audits
  • Limited employee access to data

Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your Rights

  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
